# Field Onboarding Kit — First 100 Dealers
**Offer: 1 month free, then ₹1,000/month**

---

## The 30-second pitch (say this)

"We're onboarding the first 100 EV/used-car dealers in [city] onto EvCRM —
a free dashboard for managing your leads, inventory, and customer quotes.

First month is completely free, no card needed. After that it's ₹1,000 a
month — cancel anytime. Takes 2 minutes to set up right now on your phone."

---

## What to show them (on your phone, live)

1. Open `evcrm.in/register`
2. Walk through the 3 steps with them watching:
   - Name, email, phone, password
   - Business name, GSTIN (optional — they can skip it)
   - Preview screen shows their dealership card
3. Hit "Launch My Dashboard" — they land straight in the real dashboard
4. Show them 2 things that make it click:
   - **Leads tab** — where buyer inquiries show up
   - **BuildPrice** — the live price calculator (picks their vehicle + customer's
     state, shows instant on-road price with subsidy + EMI) — this is the
     one feature that visibly impresses people fast

Takes under 5 minutes total, they're registered before you leave.

---

## What to say if they hesitate

**"What's the catch?"**
No catch — first 100 dealers only, we're building out our dealer network in
[city] and want early feedback. After 100 slots, new dealers won't get this
rate.

**"Do I need to give payment info now?"**
No. Free for the first month, nothing charged. We'll follow up before month
2 to set up billing — you can also just stop using it, no penalty.

**"Is my data safe?"**
Link them to `evcrm.in/privacy` if they ask — it's a real, specific policy,
not boilerplate.

## Important — what's real vs. what's manual right now

- ✅ **Registration is real** — dealers who sign up get a genuine working account and dashboard, immediately.
- ✅ **1 month free** — no billing is triggered at signup regardless, so this is automatically true.
- ⚠️ **₹1,000/month after month 1 is NOT automatically charged** — there's no payment gateway wired yet. This is a manual commitment for now: track who agreed to it (the sheet above), and billing has to be set up separately before month 2 for any given dealer. Don't imply to a dealer that a card will be auto-charged — it won't be, on purpose, until we build that.
- If a dealer asks for a receipt/invoice right now — there isn't an automated one yet. Take their info, tell them you'll follow up.
