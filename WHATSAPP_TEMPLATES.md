## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Best Time to Send**: 9-10 AM, 2-3 PM, 7-8 PM (India time)
2. **Group Size**: Max 256 per broadcast (WhatsApp limit)
3. **Frequency**: 1-2x per week max (avoid fatigue)
4. **Personalization**: Use "Hi [Dealer Name]" to increase open rate
5. **Call-to-Action**: Make links obvious, use emoji for visibility
6. **Follow-up**: Send second message to non-clickers after 3 days
